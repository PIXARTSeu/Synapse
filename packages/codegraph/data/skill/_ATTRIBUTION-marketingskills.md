## Skills imported (net-new only)

Only skills with **no existing local equivalent** were imported. The 16 upstream
skills whose names collided with locally-customized versions (`copywriting`,
`ab-testing`, `seo-audit`, `cold-email`, …) were **deliberately not imported**,
to preserve the local Next.js/B2B adaptations and the custom `seo-audit`
orchestrator.

| Skill | Skill | Skill |
|-------|-------|-------|
| marketing-plan | marketing-council | marketing-loops |
| prospecting | customer-research | offers |
| lead-magnets | directory-submissions | aso |
| sms | co-marketing | community-marketing |
| influencer-marketing | public-relations | free-tools |
| attribution | ads | |

> **Note on cross-references:** these upstream skills refer to sibling skills by
> their *upstream flat names* (e.g. "see emails", "see pricing", "see cro").
> Locally those concepts live under different names (`email-sequence`,
> `pricing-strategy`, `cro-patterns`, …). The prose cross-references were left as
> the original MIT text — they are guidance only and do not affect `skill_route`.
